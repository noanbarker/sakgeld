Pricing questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new family gets 14 days free. After that, you pay the price shown for your region.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel whenever you like and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

What currency am I charged in?

You will be billed based on the pricing and currency set for your region.

What payment methods do you support?

We support all major global payment methods. The exact list of available payment methods available to you will depend on your geographic location and the currency being used.
