How it works

Up and running in five minutes.

Three simple steps for you, one happy routine for your kids.

1

Setup: Add Kids & Chores

  • Choose your preferred system: Amount Per Chore, or Fixed Amount Per Cycle
  • Select your home currency
  • Create a profile for each child, with their own colour, avatar and PIN
  • Add chores from the library
2

Kids tick off their day

Each child logs in with their PIN, sees today's chores with friendly pictures, and taps "Done!" as they finish. Simple enough for the youngest, satisfying for everyone.

3

You approve, they grow

Approve completed chores from your dashboard, one at a time or a whole day at once. Balances update, milestones unlock, and the money tree grows.
