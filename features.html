Pocket money, automated

Every approved chore adds to your child's balance automatically. Kids can choose to have cash paid out to them or for you to add it to their bank account, Sprout keeps a clear, running ledger for each child.

And all the essentials, too

You stay in control

Children can mark chores as complete, but their balance only changes when you approve the work. You stay in control without having to manage every task in the moment.

Bonuses for saving

Set savings milestones to encourage saving.
e.g. Reach R1000$100, earn a R100$10 bonus.

Reconcile with real money

Quickly compare Sprout with each child’s cash or bank balance and correct any differences. Your records stay accurate, even when money changes hands outside the app.

Made for the whole family

Each child gets their own colour, avatar, PIN, chores and tree, all managed from one parent dashboard.

Rewards for achievements

Add rewards for those special achievements worth celebrating.
e.g. Achieve 90% for maths in Q2, earn a reward of R250$25.

Private & secure

No selling of family data, ever. Kids sign in with a simple PIN, parents with a secure account.
